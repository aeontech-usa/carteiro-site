Carteiro

A mail and calendar app for the desktop, by Aeon Tech

Carteiro is an email client in the spirit of Outlook: iCloud, Gmail, Outlook.com and other accounts side by side, with labels, rules, a unified inbox, follow-up reminders and a calendar.

Your mail stays yours.

Carteiro runs on your computer and talks directly to your mail and calendar providers. There is no Carteiro server in between: your messages, events and sign-ins never pass through Aeon Tech.

Signing in with Google

For Gmail accounts, Carteiro uses Google's own sign-in, so you never type your Google password into the app. It asks for access to your Gmail (to read, organize and send mail) and your Google Calendar (to show and change your events). You can remove that access at any time in your Google Account.
