Privacy policy

Carteiro, by Aeon Tech · Last updated October 27, 2026

What Carteiro is

Carteiro is a desktop application that shows, organizes and sends your email and calendar events. It runs entirely on your own computer and connects directly to the providers of the accounts you add (for example Apple iCloud, Google and Microsoft).

What data Carteiro uses

  • Your mail and calendar: messages, folders, labels and events of the accounts you add, so the app can show them to you and act on your instructions (read, reply, move, delete, label, schedule).
  • Your sign-in: an app-specific password, or for Google and Microsoft a sign-in token. These are stored only in your operating system's keychain (for example the macOS Keychain).
  • Your settings: labels, rules, colors and preferences, stored in files on your computer.

What Carteiro does not do

  • Carteiro has no server: none of your data is sent to Aeon Tech or to anyone else.
  • No analytics, no advertising, no tracking. Carteiro blocks tracking images in the mail you receive.
  • Your data is never sold, shared, or used to train any model.

Google user data

When you sign in with Google, Carteiro asks for access to Gmail (https://mail.google.com/) and Google Calendar (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar). This access is used only to provide the features you use in the app: showing, organizing and sending your mail, and showing, creating, changing and deleting your calendar events. Data received from Google stays on your computer and is not transferred to anyone.

Carteiro's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing your data

Removing an account in Carteiro deletes its saved sign-in from your keychain and its copies of messages from your computer. You can also revoke Carteiro's access at any time in your Google Account or your Microsoft account. Your mail itself stays with your provider.
